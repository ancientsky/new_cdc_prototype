# MMR vaccine (measles, mumps and rubella)（MMR vaccine）

> 權責單位：Division of Acute Infectious Diseases · 最後審閱：2026-05-14 · 下次審閱：2026-11-14 · 授權：OGDL-1.0 · ID：vaccine.mmr

MMR protects against measles, mumps and rubella. Children get one dose at 12 months and one between age 5 and school entry; adults born in 1966 or later without immunity should be assessed before travel.

## 公費對象
- 幼兒第 1 劑：出生滿 12 個月接種 1 劑
- 幼兒第 2 劑：滿 5 歲至入國小前接種 1 劑
- 產後婦女（德國麻疹抗體陰性）：產後儘速接種 1 劑（產檢德國麻疹抗體陰性者）
- 1966 年（含）以後出生、不具免疫力且前往流行地區之成人：出國前 2–4 週至旅遊醫學門診評估接種 1 劑（自費；依 2025-04-16 修訂之接種建議）

## 時程

MMR is a live attenuated vaccine; two doses are about 97% effective against measles.

Under the recommendations revised on 16 April 2025, adults **born in 1966 or later** without measles immunity who plan to visit affected areas should see a travel clinic 2 to 4 weeks before departure to be assessed for one self-paid dose; health workers and people in frequent contact with foreign nationals should also be assessed.

## 注意事項

孕婦及嚴重免疫不全者不可接種；育齡女性接種後 4 週內應避免懷孕。近期接受免疫球蛋白或輸血者需間隔一段時間再接種。
