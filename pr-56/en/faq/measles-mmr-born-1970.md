# I was born in 1970. Do I need an MMR vaccine before travelling?

> 權責單位：急性傳染病組 · 最後審閱：2026-05-14 · 下次審閱：2026-11-14 · 授權：OGDL-1.0 · ID：faq.measles-mmr-born-1970 · 依據：doc.mmr-recommendation

Possibly. Under the "Current MMR vaccination recommendations" revised on 16 April 2025, adults **born in 1966 or later** without measles immunity who plan to visit affected areas should see a travel clinic **2 to 4 weeks before departure** to be assessed for one self-paid MMR dose.

Being born in 1970 puts you within this group. You are considered immune if you have had 2 doses, a positive antibody test or confirmed measles in the past.
