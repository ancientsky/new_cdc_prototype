# Taiwan CDC signs a public health cooperation MOU with a New Southbound partner

> 發布日：2026-09-15 · 承辦單位：Division of Planning and Coordination · 最後審閱：2026-09-15 · 授權：OGDL-1.0 · ID：news.2026-09-15-international-health-mou

Taiwan CDC signed a memorandum of understanding (MOU) on public health cooperation with a partner institution in Southeast Asia under the New Southbound Policy.

The MOU covers tuberculosis and dengue control, laboratory training, migrant worker health and the timely exchange of information on cross-border events through IHR channels.

The cooperation will be reviewed each year, and its status is listed on the bilateral cooperation page.
