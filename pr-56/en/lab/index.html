Laboratories and accredited institutions

Taiwan CDC laboratory

Certified laboratory
Institutions accredited by the agency to run specific infectious-disease tests
Hospital laboratory
Tests run in the hospital itself (e.g. rapid tests, routine culture)
Regional center laboratory
Regional testing and support by the regional centers
