# Should I worry about measles when travelling to Japan?

> 權責單位：檢疫組 · 最後審閱：2026-05-14 · 下次審閱：2026-11-14 · 授權：OGDL-1.0 · ID：faq.travel-japan-measles · 依據：doc.mmr-recommendation

Since 11 May 2026 Japan has been at **Level 1 (Watch)** for measles, meaning there is measles activity and usual precautions apply.

Before departure, check your measles immunity: if you were **born in 1966 or later** and have not had 2 doses or are unsure, see a travel clinic 2–4 weeks before departure to be assessed for one self-paid MMR dose. Infants under 1 should not travel there unless necessary. If you develop fever or rash within 18 days of return, wear a mask, see a doctor and mention your travel.
