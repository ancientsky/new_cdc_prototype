# Who can get this season's government-funded flu vaccine, and when does it start?

> 權責單位：急性傳染病組 · 最後審閱：2026-09-18 · 下次審閱：2027-03-18 · 授權：OGDL-1.0 · ID：faq.flu-vaccine-who · 依據：doc.flu-vaccine-schedule

Under the 2026–27 government-funded influenza vaccination programme:

- **From 1 October 2026 (phase 1)**: adults 65+, indigenous people 55+, children from 6 months to school entry, pregnant women and parents of infants under 6 months, high-risk adults aged 19–64 (chronic illness, BMI ≥ 30, rare diseases, catastrophic illness), primary to senior high school students, health and public health workers, and long-term care residents and staff.
- **From 1 November 2026 (phase 2)**: adults aged 50–64 without high-risk conditions.

Eligible groups may change each year; follow the current season's programme.
