# English publications: Taiwan Epidemiology Bulletin and Annual Report

> 權責單位：企劃組 · 最後審閱：2026-10-01 · 下次審閱：2027-10-01 · 授權：OGDL-1.0 · ID：page.international-publications-en

Taiwan CDC publishes key surveillance results and program reports in English.

- **Taiwan Epidemiology Bulletin** — peer-reviewed reports on outbreak investigations, surveillance and program evaluation; latest issue: [Vol. 42, No. 18](/publications/bulletin-42-18/).
- **Taiwan CDC Annual Report** — the year's disease control programs and achievements; [2025 edition](/publications/annual-report-2025/).
- **Statistics of Communicable Diseases and Surveillance Report** — [annual statistics](/publications/statistics-annual-2025/).
- **Open data** — notifiable disease statistics as [open datasets](/data/).

All publications list their owner, review date and licence (Open Government Data License, version 1.0).
