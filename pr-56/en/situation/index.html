What the statuses mean

Four fixed levels, published by people
What the statuses mean
StatusMeaningTypical basis for publication
StableIndicators are within the usual range with no sustained rise.Not above baseline for 4 consecutive weeks
RisingIndicators rise for consecutive weeks but have not crossed the epidemic threshold.Rising for 3–4 weeks and below the epidemic threshold
PeakIndicators exceed the epidemic threshold and stay high.Above the epidemic threshold
DecliningIndicators fall for consecutive weeks from the high point.Falling 3 consecutive weeks from the peak
Published by
Epidemic Intelligence Center · 疫情中心狀態發布負責人

Rule
Status can only be entered by the Epidemic Intelligence Center. When any pinned disease is at "Peak", the home page switches to the situational layout.
