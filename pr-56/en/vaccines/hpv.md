# Human papillomavirus (HPV) vaccine（Human papillomavirus (HPV) vaccine）

> 權責單位：Division of Acute Infectious Diseases · 最後審閱：2026-08-20 · 下次審閱：2027-02-20 · 授權：OGDL-1.0 · ID：vaccine.hpv

Government-funded HPV vaccine is offered to first-year junior high students (boys included from the 2025 school year): two doses 6 months apart at school, protecting against cervical and other HPV-related cancers.

## 公費對象
- 國中一年級女生：2 劑，間隔 6 個月，校園集中接種
- 國中一年級男生：2 劑，間隔 6 個月，校園集中接種（自 113 學年度入學之國中生起納入，2025 年 9 月開打）

## 時程

HPV infection is linked to cervical, anal and oropharyngeal cancers. Vaccination works best before sexual activity begins.

The government-funded programme covers **first-year junior high school students**, mainly at school: two doses 6 months apart; students who miss school sessions can catch up at contracted clinics. Others can pay for vaccination; those starting at 15 or older need 3 doses.

## 注意事項

對疫苗成分過敏者不可接種；孕婦暫不建議接種。接種後少數人可能暈厥，建議接種後坐著休息 15 分鐘。
