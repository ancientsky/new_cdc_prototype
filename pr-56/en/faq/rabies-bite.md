# What should I do if I am bitten by a dog, cat or wild animal?

> 權責單位：急性傳染病組 · 最後審閱：2026-09-28 · 下次審閱：2027-03-28 · 授權：OGDL-1.0 · ID：faq.rabies-bite · 依據：disease.rabies

Remember **"wash, identify, seek care, observe"**:

1. **Wash** the wound with soap and plenty of water for 15 minutes, then disinfect with povidone-iodine.
2. **Identify** the animal's appearance and where it was.
3. **Seek care** promptly; a doctor will assess the need for rabies vaccine and immunoglobulin.
4. **Observe** the animal for 10 days if it can be safely confined.

Bites from wild ferret-badgers or masked palm civets in Taiwan, or animal bites abroad, should always be assessed for post-exposure vaccination.
