# Do healthcare workers need the MMR vaccine?

> 權責單位：感染管制及生物安全組 · 最後審閱：2026-05-14 · 下次審閱：2026-11-14 · 授權：OGDL-1.0 · ID：faq.healthcare-workers-mmr · 依據：doc.mmr-recommendation、doc.infection-control-healthcare

They should check. **Healthcare workers** are at high risk of measles exposure, and under the current MMR recommendations and the infection-control guideline for healthcare facilities they should be immune to measles: two documented MMR doses, a positive antibody test or past confirmed measles.

Those without immunity are advised to get MMR; new staff should have measles, rubella, varicella and hepatitis B immunity recorded on hiring. Non-immune staff should not care for measles patients.
