# Where can I find infectious disease statistics?

> 權責單位：疫情中心 · 最後審閱：2026-08-31 · 下次審閱：2027-03-03 · 授權：OGDL-1.0 · ID：faq.stats-where-data · 依據：dataset.nidss

You can use:

- **NIDSS (Taiwan National Infectious Disease Statistics System)**: case counts of notifiable diseases by disease, year, city or county and age.
- **Taiwan CDC open data portal**: CSV, JSON and APIs, such as daily dengue cases and influenza-like illness visits.
- **Influenza Express** and weekly epidemic reports: key surveillance points for influenza, enterovirus and more.

This site's "Open data and statistics" page lists common datasets with data date, owner and licence.
