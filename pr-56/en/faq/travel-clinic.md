# What does a travel clinic do and how early should I go before travelling?

> 權責單位：檢疫組 · 最後審閱：2026-06-15 · 下次審閱：2026-12-15 · 授權：OGDL-1.0 · ID：faq.travel-clinic · 依據：vaccine.mmr

Travel clinics assess your **destination, itinerary and health** and advise on vaccines (such as hepatitis A, yellow fever, rabies, MMR and Japanese encephalitis), malaria prevention medicine, and preparation for altitude sickness and travellers' diarrhoea.

Visit **4 to 6 weeks before departure** so vaccines have time to work; last-minute travellers can still be assessed. A list of contracted clinics is on the Taiwan CDC website.
