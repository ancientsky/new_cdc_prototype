# I had a flu vaccine last year. Do I need another one this year?

> 權責單位：急性傳染病組 · 最後審閱：2026-09-18 · 下次審閱：2027-03-18 · 授權：OGDL-1.0 · ID：faq.flu-vaccine-every-year · 依據：vaccine.influenza

Yes. Influenza viruses change from year to year and the WHO updates the vaccine strains annually based on surveillance. Protection also wanes after about 6 months, **so vaccination is needed every year**.

Protection develops about 2 weeks after vaccination, so get it early before the season peaks.
