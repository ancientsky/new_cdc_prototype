# 2026–27 government-funded flu vaccination starts 1 October, with COVID-19 vaccine available at the same time

> 發布日：2026-09-18 · 承辦單位：Division of Acute Infectious Diseases · 最後審閱：2026-09-18 · 授權：OGDL-1.0 · ID：news.2026-09-18-flu-vaccine-oct1

Taiwan CDC announced that government-funded flu vaccination for 2026–27 starts on **1 October**. Phase 1 covers adults 65+, indigenous people 55+, children from 6 months to school entry, pregnant women and parents of infants under 6 months, high-risk adults aged 19–64, primary to senior high school students, health and public health workers, and long-term care residents and staff. Adults aged 50–64 without high-risk conditions are eligible from **1 November**.

The new-season COVID-19 vaccine starts the same day for everyone aged 6 months and over and can be given together with the flu vaccine. Find a clinic via the influenza portal or the vaccination map.
