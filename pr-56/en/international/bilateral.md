## New Southbound health cooperation
Under the New Southbound Policy, Taiwan CDC cooperates with partners in South and Southeast Asia on tuberculosis, dengue and other vector-borne diseases, migrant worker health and laboratory capacity, and welcomes their health officials to training programs in Taiwan.

> Partner topics and statuses are illustrative in this prototype, and signing years are left blank until the Division of International Cooperation confirms them (dates are recorded by year when published).
