Vaccines

Influenza vaccine

Influenza vaccine

Government-funded flu vaccination for 2026–27 starts on 1 October for older adults, young children, pregnant women, high-risk groups and students; adults aged 50–64 from 1 November. Get vaccinated every year.

Last reviewed Sep 18, 2026

Influenza

MMR vaccine (measles, mumps and rubella)

MMR vaccine

MMR protects against measles, mumps and rubella. Children get one dose at 12 months and one between age 5 and school entry; adults born in 1966 or later without immunity should be assessed before travel.

Last reviewed May 14, 2026

Measles 德國麻疹

COVID-19 vaccine

COVID-19 vaccine

The 2026–27 COVID-19 vaccine starts on 1 October alongside flu vaccine: one government-funded dose for everyone aged 6 months and over; people 65+ and the immunocompromised may get another dose 6 months later.

Last reviewed Sep 16, 2026

COVID-19

腸病毒 71 型疫苗

EV71 vaccine

國內已核准上市的腸病毒 71 型疫苗屬自費接種，適用年齡依仿單；只能預防 EV71，接種後仍需落實洗手。

Last reviewed Apr 22, 2026

Human papillomavirus (HPV) vaccine

HPV vaccine

Government-funded HPV vaccine is offered to first-year junior high students (boys included from the 2025 school year): two doses 6 months apart at school, protecting against cervical and other HPV-related cancers.

Last reviewed Aug 20, 2026

肺炎鏈球菌疫苗

Pneumococcal vaccine

幼兒 2、4、12–15 個月公費接種結合型疫苗；成人公費自 2026 年 1 月 15 日起改為 1 劑 20 價結合型疫苗（PCV20），8 月 10 日起增加 21 價（PCV21）可選，對象為 65 歲以上長者、55–64 歲原住民與 19–64 歲侵襲性肺炎鏈球菌感染症高風險對象。

Last reviewed Oct 5, 2026

Hepatitis B vaccine

新生兒公費接種 3 劑 B 型肝炎疫苗：出生 24 小時內、滿 1 個月、滿 6 個月；母親 HBsAg 陽性者另於 24 小時內注射 HBIG。

Last reviewed Apr 15, 2026

幼兒出生滿 12 個月公費接種 1 劑水痘疫苗；4–6 歲可自費接種第 2 劑，未感染過的成人可自費 2 劑。
