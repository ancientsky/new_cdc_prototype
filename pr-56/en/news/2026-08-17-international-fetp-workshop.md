# International field epidemiology workshop opens in Taipei

> 發布日：2026-08-17 · 承辦單位：Division of Planning and Coordination · 最後審閱：2026-08-17 · 授權：OGDL-1.0 · ID：news.2026-08-17-international-fetp-workshop

Taiwan CDC opened an international field epidemiology workshop in Taipei for public health officials and trainees from partner countries.

The workshop combines Taiwan's Field Epidemiology Training Program (FETP) experience with practical sessions on outbreak investigation, dengue and vector control, laboratory diagnosis and risk communication. Participants also visit a local health bureau and a quarantine station.

Foreign health officials interested in future sessions can apply through the online international training service.
