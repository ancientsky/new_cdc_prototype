Applications

Data requests, laboratory requests, vaccination certificates, vaccine fund donations, travel clinics and more. Each lists steps, documents, processing time and legal basis.

  • Members of the public (7)
  • Medical institutions (7)
  • Researchers (3)
  • Local health units (3)

Members of the public

  • Report a suspected outbreak (public, schools, facilities)

    If several people at the same place fall ill with similar symptoms within a short time, call 1922 or the local health bureau for investigation and testing.

    Notification 1 working days Fee：免費

  • 醫療與長照機構感染管制查核申訴與諮詢

    機構對感染管制查核結果有異議可於收受通知後 30 日內申請複核；民眾發現機構感染管制疏失可向衛生局或疾管署陳情，處理期間約 30 日。

    Other 30 working days Fee：免費

  • Apply for Taiwan CDC international training

    Foreign health officials and professionals can apply online for Taiwan CDC international training (FETP exchange, workshops, laboratory, TB-DOTS and dengue control); applications are reviewed within 30 days.

    Other 30 working days Fee：Training is free of charge; travel and accommodation arrangements depend on the program.

  • International Certificate of Vaccination (yellow card)

    Travellers to countries requiring yellow fever vaccination get vaccinated and receive the certificate at travel medicine clinics; it becomes valid 10 days after vaccination.

    Certificate 1 working days Fee：疫苗費、證明書工本費與掛號費依各院所公告（自費）

  • Book a travel medicine clinic

    Book a contracted travel medicine clinic 4–6 weeks before departure for vaccines, malaria prophylaxis, the yellow card and pre-travel advice.

    Clinic 7 working days Fee：掛號費與自費疫苗、藥品費用依各院所公告

  • 預防接種紀錄與接種證明補發

    兒童預防接種紀錄卡遺失或入學、出國需要接種證明時，可至衛生所或原接種院所申請補發，也可透過健康存摺查詢下載接種紀錄。

    Certificate 5 working days Fee：紀錄卡補發免費；英文證明工本費依各單位公告

  • Donate to the Vaccine Fund

    Individuals and companies can donate by postal transfer, bank transfer or in person; donations fund publicly funded vaccines and receipts are tax-deductible.

    Donation 14 working days Fee：免手續費（劃撥／匯款手續費由捐款人自行負擔）

Medical institutions

  • Request for notifiable disease case data (research)

    Researchers may request de-identified case data with a research protocol and IRB approval; review takes about 30 days.

    Data request 30 working days Fee：依資料專區使用規費標準收費（示意）

  • 醫療與長照機構感染管制查核申訴與諮詢

    機構對感染管制查核結果有異議可於收受通知後 30 日內申請複核；民眾發現機構感染管制疏失可向衛生局或疾管署陳情，處理期間約 30 日。

    Other 30 working days Fee：免費

  • Laboratory testing for notifiable diseases (specimens from healthcare facilities)

    After notification, facilities collect specimens per lab specifications and send them with a requisition form; results are returned via NIDRS.

    Lab request 5 working days Fee：法定傳染病確認檢驗免費

  • 潛伏結核感染檢驗與治療

    結核病接觸者與計畫對象可接受公費 IGRA 檢驗與潛伏結核感染治療（3HP、1HP、3HR、4R、9H），治療期間由關懷員陪伴服藥（DOPT）。

    Clinic 14 working days Fee：免費（公費支應）

  • Notifiable disease reporting (physicians and facilities)

    Physicians must report suspected notifiable diseases via NIDRS within category-specific deadlines (24 hours for categories 1–2, one week for most category 3).

    Notification 1 working days Fee：免費

  • 傳染病防治醫療網應變醫院指定與隔離病床申請

    醫院申請成為傳染病防治醫療網應變醫院或設置負壓隔離病床，須符合設施、人員與感染管制基準，經區管中心實地審查與指定，處理期間約 60 日。

    License 60 working days Fee：免費

  • 結核病診治費用補助與隔離治療

    結核病個案就醫由健保給付、部分負擔由疾管署支應；多重抗藥性結核病由 TMTC 收治並由公費支應；依法施行隔離治療者，費用由政府負擔。

    Other 7 working days Fee：免費（部分負擔由疾管署支應；隔離治療費用由政府負擔）

Researchers

  • Request for notifiable disease case data (research)

    Researchers may request de-identified case data with a research protocol and IRB approval; review takes about 30 days.

    Data request 30 working days Fee：依資料專區使用規費標準收費（示意）

  • Media interview and filming requests

    Media should apply to the Public Relations Office at least three working days in advance, stating the topic, desired expertise and publication date.

    Other 3 working days Fee：免費

  • Request for research project reports and data

    How to request full reports, datasets and code from commissioned or in-house research projects; processing takes about 20 days.

    Data request 20 working days Fee：免費
