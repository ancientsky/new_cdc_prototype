Who can get the government-funded mpox vaccine and how many doses are needed?

Public versionApplies to: Taiwan

Government-funded mpox (formerly monkeypox) vaccine is offered to people with high-risk sexual behaviour in the past year, such as those with multiple partners, sex workers, or people who have had sex at commercial venues, and to close contacts assessed by health authorities.

It requires 2 doses at least 4 weeks apart for full protection. After contact with a case, get vaccinated ideally within 4 days and no later than 14 days.
