# Measles rising in Japan: Level 1 travel notice; assess MMR before departure

> 發布日：2026-05-11 · 承辦單位：Division of Quarantine · 最後審閱：2026-05-11 · 授權：OGDL-1.0 · ID：news.2026-05-11-japan-measles-level1

Measles cases in Japan are markedly higher than in previous years, with clusters in several prefectures. Taiwan CDC has placed Japan at **Level 1: Watch** for measles; travellers should follow usual precautions.

Under the current MMR recommendations, people **born in 1966 or later** without measles immunity who plan to visit affected areas such as Japan should see a travel clinic 2 to 4 weeks before departure to be assessed for one self-paid MMR dose; infants under 1 should not travel there unless necessary. Anyone who develops fever or rash within 18 days of return should wear a mask, see a doctor promptly and mention their travel.
