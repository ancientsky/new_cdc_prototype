# What should I do if I get a fever after returning from abroad?

> 權責單位：檢疫組 · 最後審閱：2026-06-15 · 下次審閱：2026-12-15 · 授權：OGDL-1.0 · ID：faq.travel-return-fever · 依據：disease.dengue

Wear a mask, see a doctor promptly and **tell them which countries you visited, how long you stayed and any contacts**. If you feel unwell on arrival, you can report it to airport quarantine officers.

Fever after travel may be dengue, measles, malaria or influenza, and the doctor will order tests based on your travel history. If you visited a dengue-affected area, keep avoiding mosquito bites for two weeks after return.
