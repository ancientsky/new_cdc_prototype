# What are the warning signs of severe enterovirus infection?

> 權責單位：急性傳染病組 · 最後審閱：2026-04-22 · 下次審閱：2026-10-22 · 授權：OGDL-1.0 · ID：faq.ev-severe-signs · 依據：disease.enterovirus

If a child under 5 with enterovirus shows **drowsiness, confusion, poor activity, limb weakness, myoclonic jerks** (sudden startles or whole-body jerks), **persistent vomiting, rapid breathing or a fast heartbeat**, go to a major hospital immediately.

Severe complications usually appear 2 to 7 days after onset, so parents should watch closely during this period.
