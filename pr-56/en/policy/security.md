# Information security policy

> 權責單位：資訊室 · 最後審閱：2026-10-10 · 下次審閱：2027-10-10 · 授權：OGDL-1.0 · ID：page.security-policy

> **Illustrative draft pending confirmation by the IT Office.** This page follows the common structure of an agency information security management system (ISMS). It does not claim any certification or that every measure is in place; the IT Office must rewrite it to match the actual system and security responsibility level.

## 1. Purpose
To protect the confidentiality, integrity and availability of the information assets Taiwan CDC holds (outbreak data, personal data and system services) and keep operations running during security incidents.

## 2. Scope
All staff and contractors, and all information systems, networks, data and paper records of the agency.

## 3. Policy statements
1. **Risk-based:** assets are inventoried and risks assessed regularly; controls follow the risk.
2. **Classification:** information is protected by sensitivity; personal data and unpublished outbreak data receive the strictest controls.
3. **Least privilege:** access follows job duties, is reviewed regularly and revoked promptly on departure or transfer.
4. **Outsourcing:** contractors sign confidentiality and security clauses and are subject to audit.
5. **Incident reporting and response:** incidents are reported at once to the IT Office and onward within the required time limits, with records kept.
6. **Training:** all staff receive annual security training.
7. **Continual improvement:** regular internal audits and management reviews.

## 4. Legal basis
The Cyber Security Management Act and its regulations, the Personal Data Protection Act and related government security rules; see the [national legal database](https://law.moj.gov.tw/).

## 5. Responsibilities
The Director-General oversees security management; the IT Office runs the system and technical controls; unit heads are responsible for their areas; all staff must comply.

## 6. Reporting a concern
If you find a vulnerability on this site, tell us through [Contact us](/contact/) and please do not publish the details.
