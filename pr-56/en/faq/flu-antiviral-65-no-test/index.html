Does someone aged 65+ with flu-like symptoms need a positive rapid test to get government-funded antivirals?

Public versionApplies to: Taiwan

Reviewed translation

Responsible unit Division of Acute Infectious Diseases Last reviewed Oct 5, 2026 Next review Apr 5, 2027 This page can be cited by AI · structured data available

No. Under Article 4 of the year-round eligibility criteria for government-funded influenza antivirals, children under 5 and adults aged 65 or older with influenza-like illness can be prescribed antivirals directly once a physician judges them eligible; no rapid test is required. The same applies to the other year-round groups (pregnant women, hospitalised patients, people with high-risk chronic conditions).

From 24 August to 31 October 2026 (extended by Letter to the Medical Community No. 616), people with flu-like symptoms in seven high-transmission groups are also eligible without a rapid test. Check Taiwan CDC's latest announcements for the current rules.
