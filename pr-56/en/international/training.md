# International training: FETP, workshops and laboratory training

> 權責單位：企劃組 · 最後審閱：2026-10-01 · 下次審閱：2027-10-01 · 授權：OGDL-1.0 · ID：page.international-training

Taiwan CDC shares its field and laboratory experience with health professionals from partner countries.

| Program | For | Format | Frequency |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Field Epidemiology Training Program (FETP) exchange | Field epidemiologists and trainees | Joint investigations, case studies, short attachments | Each year (schedule illustrative) |
| International workshops (incl. GCTF) | Public health officials and clinicians | 3–5 day workshops on topics such as dengue, AMR and emerging infections | Several sessions per year (illustrative) |
| Laboratory training | Laboratory staff | Hands-on training in diagnostic methods and biosafety | On request |
| TB control and DOTS | TB program managers | Visits to directly observed therapy (DOTS) programs and contact investigation | On request |
| Dengue control workshop | Vector-control and surveillance staff | Source reduction, vector surveillance and cluster response | Before the dengue season (illustrative) |

## How to apply
Foreign health officials apply through the online service [Apply for Taiwan CDC international training](/apply/international-training-application/). Applications are usually endorsed by the applicant's agency or a partner representative office.
