# Does the MMR vaccine cause autism?

> 權責單位：急性傳染病組 · 最後審閱：2026-05-14 · 下次審閱：2026-11-14 · 授權：OGDL-1.0 · ID：faq.rumor-mmr-autism · 依據：vaccine.mmr

**No.** The claim comes from a 1998 paper that was later retracted, and its author lost his medical licence. Large studies in many countries covering millions of children have since confirmed there is no link between MMR and autism.

Skipping the vaccine leaves children exposed to measles, which can cause pneumonia, encephalitis and even death.
