# Privacy policy (revised 2026)

> 權責單位：資訊室 · 最後審閱：2026-10-10 · 下次審閱：2027-10-10 · 授權：OGDL-1.0 · ID：page.privacy

> **The sections below describe the production site plan. This prototype runs no web analytics, and your Q&A history stays only in your own browser.**

Planned for production: server logs (kept 6 months); **Google Analytics 4 (GA4)** statistics that do **not** record or store IP addresses (the old Universal Analytics "IP anonymisation" setting no longer applies); and **de-identified AI Q&A logs** (personal identifiers masked first; kept 12 months; never used for training). In the prototype nothing is stored server-side: Q&A history and feedback drafts live only in your browser. Share buttons connect to LINE or Facebook **only when clicked**. Email newsletter (planned for production; the prototype sends nothing): we keep only your address, chosen topics, frequency, language and consent time, use them only to send what you subscribed to, require a confirmation click (double opt-in), add a one-click unsubscribe link to every message, keep only the address and an unsubscribed flag after you leave, and delete unconfirmed requests within 7 days. In the prototype this data stays in your browser or in the local development folder `.local/`; the mail service provider and retention period will be set before launch. RSS and the LINE official account do not pass through this site. Language and view preferences are stored only in your browser. The advanced bring-your-own-key AI mode is a demo limited to staff; the key is kept only for the current browser session (sessionStorage), cleared when the tab or browser is closed, and never sent to this site. You may request access, correction or deletion of personal data under the Personal Data Protection Act via the contact form or the 1922 hotline. Revised September 2026; corrected 10 October 2026.
