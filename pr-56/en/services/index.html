Applications and services

Eight common entry points for professionals, researchers and media. Members of the public can simply start from the question box on the home page.

  • ReportingNotifiable diseases · time limits
  • Lab testingSpecimens · containers · submission
  • Campaign materialsVideos · posters · health education
  • StatisticsOutbreak statistics · open data
  • ApplicationsData · testing · certificates
  • ResearchProjects · reports
  • PublicationsBulletins · annual reports · manuals
  • Vaccine fundDonations and use

The public home page only carries a “More services” text line, not these icons: early research showed the public has no mental model of an “applications area”.

Topics and resources

  • 抗蛇毒血清與毒蛇咬傷急救被蛇咬傷請當作毒蛇咬傷處理、保持冷靜、儘速就醫，緊急請撥 119。臺灣六種常見毒蛇與疾管署供應的 4 種抗蛇毒血清對照、哪裡有血清、給醫療人員的取得與調度方式。
  • Disaster and evacuation: disease prevention after disastersKey steps after typhoons, earthquakes and floods: remove standing water, keep drinking water and food safe, prevent outbreaks in shelters, and follow the national civil defence guide.
  • Antimicrobial resistance: One Health actionA One Health campaign running until 30 November 2026 alongside World AMR Awareness Week: no antibiotics for colds, finish prescribed courses, and open AMR surveillance data.Until Nov 30, 2026
  • Anonymous counselling and testing for sexually transmitted infectionsDesignated hospitals offer anonymous HIV and syphilis testing without an NHI card; HIV self-test kits are also available, and case managers link positive results to care.
  • PrEP programmeHow to start pre-exposure prophylaxis with partner hospitals: eligibility, dosing options, tests before and during PrEP, and subsidies.
  • 結核病防治專區給醫療人員與衛生局的結核病防治入口：診治指引、工作手冊、病例定義與通報、檢驗、都治、潛伏結核感染、接觸者檢查、抗藥性照護體系、高風險族群篩檢與統計。
  • Infection control in long-term care facilitiesManuals, audit criteria, cluster reporting, vaccination for residents and staff, and training materials to help long-term care facilities prevent outbreaks.
  • Health for new residents and migrant workers: information in seven languagesDisease prevention information in seven languages: migrant worker health checks, TB, dengue, vaccines and the multilingual 1922 hotline.
  • 性傳染病衛教資源彙整愛滋、梅毒、淋病、M痘等性傳染病的衛教單張、影片與校園教材，說明安全性行為、保險套與篩檢資訊，供民眾、學校與團體下載使用。
  • Vaccine Fund donationsThe statutory Vaccine Fund accepts public donations to purchase publicly funded vaccines; how to donate, receipts, tax deduction and fund disclosures.
  • International CooperationHow Taiwan CDC works with WHO and partner countries: the 24/7 IHR National Focal Point, multilateral and bilateral cooperation, international training and English publications.
