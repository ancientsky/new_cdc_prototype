## 2. Requesting government information
Information that is not proactively disclosed can be requested under the Law. During the prototype, please use [Contact us](/contact/) and describe what you need and why. Online requests and progress tracking are to be built.

## 3. Restrictions
Information involving personal privacy, trade secrets, national security or other legally confidential matters may be withheld, with reasons given in the reply.

## 4. Licence
Proactively disclosed data is released under the [open data licence](/policy/open-data/).
