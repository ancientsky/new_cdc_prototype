# Who can get the government-funded HPV vaccine?

> 權責單位：急性傳染病組 · 最後審閱：2026-10-05 · 下次審閱：2027-04-05 · 授權：OGDL-1.0 · ID：faq.hpv-vaccine-who · 依據：vaccine.hpv

The government-funded HPV vaccine is offered to **first-year junior high school students**, mainly through school vaccination; from the 2025 school year it covers boys as well as girls. **Two doses are given 6 months apart**; students who miss school sessions can catch up at contracted clinics.

Other ages can be vaccinated at their own expense; those starting at age 15 or older need 3 doses.
