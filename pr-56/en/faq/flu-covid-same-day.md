# Can I get the flu and COVID-19 vaccines on the same day?

> 權責單位：急性傳染病組 · 最後審閱：2026-09-18 · 下次審閱：2027-03-18 · 授權：OGDL-1.0 · ID：faq.flu-covid-same-day · 依據：doc.covid-vaccine-recommendation

Yes. Flu and COVID-19 vaccines can be **given at the same visit in different sites**, or at any interval, without affecting protection.

Getting both together saves trips and helps older adults and people with chronic conditions get protected before the autumn and winter season.
