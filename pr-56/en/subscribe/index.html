Subscribe & alerts

There are three ways to hear first, ordered from needing no personal data to needing the most: RSS, the LINE official account, and email.

  • RSS feeds
  • LINE official account
  • Email subscription

RSS feeds (no personal data needed)

RSS is a website's update list: add a feed address to an RSS reader and new items appear there automatically. You do not need to keep checking, and you do not give an email address or create an account.

  1. Pick an RSS reader: a phone app, a browser add-on, or the one built into your email program.
  2. Copy the address of the feed you want from the table below (one address per feed).
  3. In the reader choose Add feed and paste the address. To stop, remove the feed in the reader.

Email subscription (double opt-in)

Receive press releases, outbreak updates and other topics in your inbox. After you submit, we send a confirmation email and the subscription only starts when you click its link. Every message has a one-click unsubscribe link.

This is a prototype: no mail service has been chosen yet, so no email is really sent. The banner below says where your demo data is kept.

Personal data notice
  • Received by: Public Relations Office
  • Prototype demo: nothing is sent to Taiwan CDC, and nothing is stored on this website or in its source repository (Git).
  • Purpose: to send the alerts you subscribed to; you can unsubscribe at any time with one click.
  • Retention: kept for the period announced by the receiving unit, then deleted (announced before launch).
  • Under the Personal Data Protection Act you may ask to view, copy, correct, stop the use of, or delete your data. Required fields must be filled for us to process your request. Privacy policy

What we collect and keep
  • Only: your email address, chosen topics, frequency and language, and the time you consented. No name, phone or location.
  • Used only to send what you subscribed to, for nothing else, and not shared with third parties (except the mail service provider, under a data protection agreement).
  • Requests not confirmed within 48 hours are deleted automatically after at most 7 days.
  • After you unsubscribe we stop sending and keep only the address and an unsubscribed flag, so you are not added back by mistake.
