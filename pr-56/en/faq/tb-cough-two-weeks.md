# How long should a cough last before I suspect tuberculosis?

> 權責單位：慢性傳染病組 · 最後審閱：2026-04-01 · 下次審閱：2026-10-01 · 授權：OGDL-1.0 · ID：faq.tb-cough-two-weeks · 依據：disease.tuberculosis

If you have **coughed for more than 2 weeks**, especially with sputum, chest pain, weight loss, poor appetite, afternoon fever or night sweats, see a chest physician for a chest X-ray and sputum test.

Almost everyone with TB is cured by taking medicine regularly for at least 6 months, and early detection protects your family.
