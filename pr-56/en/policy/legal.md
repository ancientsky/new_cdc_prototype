# Laws and regulations

> 權責單位：企劃組 · 最後審閱：2026-10-10 · 下次審閱：2027-10-10 · 授權：OGDL-1.0 · ID：page.legal

> **Prototype note:** This page lists the main laws and links to the Ministry of Justice national legal database. The database is authoritative. Entries marked "to be confirmed" still need their statute codes checked by the legal affairs unit and currently link to a database search.

## 1. Communicable disease control
- [Communicable Disease Control Act](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawAll.aspx?pcode=L0050001): the parent act covering classification, notification, quarantine, control measures, vaccination and penalties.
- [Enforcement Rules of the Communicable Disease Control Act](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawAll.aspx?pcode=L0050003).
- Other regulations made under the Act: [search the database for "communicable disease control"](https://law.moj.gov.tw/Law/LawSearchResult.aspx?ty=ONEBAR&kw=%E5%82%B3%E6%9F%93%E7%97%85%E9%98%B2%E6%B2%BB) (to be confirmed).

## 2. Immunization
- [Regulations on the Levy and Review of the Vaccine Injury Compensation Fund](https://law.moj.gov.tw/LawClass/LawAll.aspx?pcode=L0050008).
- Other immunization rules: [search the database for "immunization"](https://law.moj.gov.tw/Law/LawSearchResult.aspx?ty=ONEBAR&kw=%E9%A0%90%E9%98%B2%E6%8E%A5%E7%A8%AE) (to be confirmed).
- Current vaccination recommendations are in the [document library](/documents/) and [vaccines](/vaccines/) pages; a statute is not a vaccination recommendation, so follow the current version of the recommendation.

## 3. Checking that an article is current
The legal database shows the latest amendment date and history. When citing, give the act name, article number and the date you checked. This site does not keep its own copy of statutory text.
