# How can I tell influenza from a common cold?

> 權責單位：急性傳染病組 · 最後審閱：2026-09-20 · 下次審閱：2027-03-20 · 授權：OGDL-1.0 · ID：faq.flu-vs-cold · 依據：disease.influenza

Influenza usually starts **suddenly** with marked whole-body symptoms such as fever, headache, muscle aches and extreme tiredness. A cold tends to develop gradually with a runny nose, sneezing and sore throat, rarely with high fever or body aches.

Influenza can lead to pneumonia, encephalitis or myocarditis. Older adults, young children, pregnant women and people with chronic conditions should see a doctor early if they have flu-like symptoms.
