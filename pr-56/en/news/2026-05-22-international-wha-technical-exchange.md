# Taiwan CDC experts hold technical exchanges during the World Health Assembly week

> 發布日：2026-05-22 · 承辦單位：Division of Planning and Coordination · 最後審閱：2026-05-22 · 授權：OGDL-1.0 · ID：news.2026-05-22-international-wha-technical-exchange

During the World Health Assembly (WHA) week in Geneva, Taiwan CDC experts held technical exchanges with health officials and experts from partner countries.

Topics included event-based surveillance, implementation of the International Health Regulations (IHR 2005) and its 24/7 National Focal Point, preparedness for emerging infectious diseases and antimicrobial resistance.

Taiwan CDC will continue to share its epidemic prevention experience and seek meaningful participation in WHO technical meetings.
