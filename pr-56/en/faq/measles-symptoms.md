# What are the symptoms of measles and how long is it contagious?

> 權責單位：急性傳染病組 · 最後審閱：2026-05-14 · 下次審閱：2026-11-14 · 授權：OGDL-1.0 · ID：faq.measles-symptoms · 依據：disease.measles

Measles begins with **high fever, cough, runny nose and conjunctivitis**, sometimes with white Koplik spots inside the mouth. After 3–4 days a red rash starts behind the ears and on the face and spreads over the body.

Patients are contagious **from 4 days before to 4 days after the rash appears** and the virus spreads through the air. If you have a fever with a rash, phone the hospital first, wear a mask and mention your travel history.
