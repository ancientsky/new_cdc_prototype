# Who can get a government-funded COVID-19 vaccine this season, and how many doses?

> 權責單位：急性傳染病組 · 最後審閱：2026-09-16 · 下次審閱：2027-03-16 · 授權：OGDL-1.0 · ID：faq.covid-vaccine-who · 依據：doc.covid-vaccine-recommendation

Under the COVID-19 vaccination recommendations for 2026–27 (revised September 2026):

- **Everyone aged 6 months and over** can receive one government-funded dose from 1 October 2026, at least 12 weeks after any previous dose.
- **Adults aged 65+ and people who are moderately or severely immunocompromised** may receive another dose 6 months after this season's first dose.

Last season's (2025–26) recommendation did not include the extra dose; follow the current version.
