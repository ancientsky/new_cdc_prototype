# International Cooperation

> 權責單位：Division of Planning and Coordination · 最後審閱：2026-10-01 · 下次審閱：2027-10-01 · 授權：OGDL-1.0 · ID：topic.international-cooperation

Taiwan Centers for Disease Control (Taiwan CDC) works with the World Health Organization (WHO), regional networks and partner countries so that outbreaks are detected early and information crosses borders quickly.

- **IHR National Focal Point, 24/7** — Taiwan CDC carries out the National Focal Point functions under the International Health Regulations (IHR 2005). To contact the focal point about an urgent public health event, call the Taiwan CDC hotline 1922 at any time (from abroad +886-800-001922); official correspondence goes through the switchboard +886-2-2395-9825.
- **Multilateral cooperation** — technical exchanges with WHO and during the World Health Assembly week, the APEC Health Working Group and the Global Health Security Agenda (GHSA).
- **Bilateral cooperation** — the United States, Japan, the European Union and New Southbound partners in South and Southeast Asia.
- **Training** — the Field Epidemiology Training Program (FETP), international workshops and laboratory training; foreign health officials apply online through the international training application service.
- **Publications** — the Taiwan Epidemiology Bulletin and the Taiwan CDC Annual Report in English.
