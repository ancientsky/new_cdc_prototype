# MMR recommendations revised: adults born in 1966 or later now eligible for assessment

> 發布日：2025-04-16 · 承辦單位：Division of Acute Infectious Diseases · 最後審閱：2025-04-16 · 授權：OGDL-1.0 · ID：news.2025-04-16-mmr-recommendation-revised

In response to rising measles activity worldwide, Taiwan CDC today (16 April) revised the "Current MMR vaccination recommendations", effective immediately.

Key changes: the birth-year cut-off for adult assessment moved from 1981 to **1966 or later** for people without measles immunity; travellers should be assessed at a travel clinic 2 to 4 weeks before departure for one self-paid dose; and workers in frequent contact with foreign nationals (such as airport, tourism and migrant-worker services) were added. Affected areas follow Taiwan CDC travel health notice levels.
