# How many days should a child with enterovirus stay home?

> 權責單位：急性傳染病組 · 最後審閱：2026-04-22 · 下次審閱：2026-10-22 · 授權：OGDL-1.0 · ID：faq.ev-stay-home · 依據：disease.enterovirus

A sick child should **stay home for at least 7 days** and avoid contact with other children to reduce spread.

The virus can still be shed in stool for weeks after recovery, so after the child returns to school, parents and teachers should keep washing hands with soap after toilet use and before meals.
