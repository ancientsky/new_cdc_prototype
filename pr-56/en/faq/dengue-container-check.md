# What does "inspect, empty, clean, scrub" mean for dengue prevention?

> 權責單位：急性傳染病組 · 最後審閱：2026-07-20 · 下次審閱：2027-01-20 · 授權：OGDL-1.0 · ID：faq.dengue-container-check · 依據：disease.dengue

These are four steps to remove mosquito breeding sites, done **once a week**:

1. **Inspect** containers inside and outside the home that may hold water, such as plant saucers, buckets and old tyres.
2. **Empty** the standing water.
3. **Clean** away containers you do not need.
4. **Scrub** the inside walls to remove mosquito eggs.

Aedes mosquitoes take about 7–10 days to develop from egg to adult, so weekly cleaning breaks the cycle.
