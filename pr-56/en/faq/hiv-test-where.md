# Where can I get an anonymous HIV test?

> 權責單位：慢性傳染病組 · 最後審閱：2026-06-10 · 下次審閱：2026-12-10 · 授權：OGDL-1.0 · ID：faq.hiv-test-where · 依據：disease.hiv

You can choose:

- **Anonymous testing hospitals**: no health insurance card needed; counselling and testing provided.
- **Community testing sites** run by NGOs with local health bureaus.
- **HIV self-test kits** from designated pharmacies, vending machines or online; some people can get free kits.

A positive self-test must be confirmed at a hospital. If fewer than 3 months have passed since possible exposure, test again later even if negative (window period).
