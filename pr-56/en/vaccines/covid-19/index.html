COVID-19 vaccine COVID-19 vaccine

The 2026–27 COVID-19 vaccine starts on 1 October alongside flu vaccine: one government-funded dose for everyone aged 6 months and over; people 65+ and the immunocompromised may get another dose 6 months later.

Schedule and notes

COVID-19 vaccines are updated each year as the virus changes. The 2026–27 vaccine follows WHO-recommended strains and is available from 1 October for everyone aged 6 months and over; priority groups include adults 65+, indigenous people 55+, health workers, long-term care residents and staff, high-risk chronic patients and pregnant women.

It can be given with the flu vaccine in different sites, or at any interval.

Precautions

對疫苗成分曾發生嚴重過敏反應者不可接種。接種後少數人可能出現注射部位疼痛、疲倦、頭痛、發燒；若出現胸痛、心悸、呼吸急促，應立即就醫。

Where to get vaccinated

Funded vaccines are given at contracted clinics and health centres; before travel, see a travel-medicine clinic.
