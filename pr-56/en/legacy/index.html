How old addresses are redirected

  • Pages that moved are redirected with a 301 (permanent redirect), so bookmarks and search engines follow. An old address is redirected once, never in a chain, and never to an unrelated page or the home page.
  • Content that is confirmed to be discontinued returns 410 (gone), with an explanation on the new site. Old pages still pending confirmation are not redirected until the responsible unit has checked them.
  • Retention and closing date: the “replaces N pages of the previous website” notice on new pages is shown until Dec 31, 2027 (showLegacyUntil in the mapping list) and then hides itself automatically.
  • The 301 redirects themselves do not close with the notice. They are reviewed for removal only after server 404 logs show old-address traffic has dropped to zero; the prototype suggests keeping them at least 12 months.
  • Machine-readable mapping：/v1/legacy-map.json · /v1/redirects.json
