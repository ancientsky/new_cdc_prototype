Payo sa kalusugan para sa biyahe sa Malta

歐洲 · Check the alert level, vaccines and care options before you go.

Walang partikular na travel health notice Malta · MT

Kasalukuyang antas at abiso

✓ Walang partikular na travel health notice sa ngayon

Walang partikular na travel health notice ang Malta sa ngayon. Sapat na ang karaniwang pag-iingat.

  • Wash your hands often and take care with food and drinking water.
  • Avoid mosquito bites outdoors; do not touch or feed animals.
  • If you get a fever, rash or diarrhoea after returning, see a doctor and mention your travel history.

May pangkalahatang paalala rin: COVID-19 with severe complications（Antas 1）. Sapat na ang karaniwang pag-iingat.

Tatlong yugto ng biyahe

  1. 1 Bago umalis

    • See a travel-medicine clinic 2–4 weeks before departure
    • No specific vaccine; make sure routine vaccines (such as MMR) are up to date.
    • International Certificate of Vaccination (yellow card)

  2. 2 Habang naglalakbay

    • Wash your hands often and take care with food and drinking water.
    • Avoid mosquito bites outdoors; do not touch or feed animals.

  3. 3 Pagbalik

    • Kung magkaroon ng lagnat o pantal sa loob ng 21 araw pagkabalik, magpatingin agad at sabihin ang iyong travel at contact history.
    • Tumawag sa hotline 1922 kung may tanong, at tumawag muna bago pumunta sa klinika o ospital.

For other destinations, see the Talaan ng travel health notice
