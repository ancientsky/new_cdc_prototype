# AI use statement and crawler policy

> 權責單位：AI推動辦公室 · 最後審閱：2026-09-22 · 下次審閱：2027-09-22 · 授權：OGDL-1.0 · ID：page.ai-policy

## How we use AI
The Q&A feature only **relays official content; it does not create it**. By default every sentence is extracted from reviewed official content and cited.

## Seven rules
1. Only whitelisted, current, in-review-period content is used.
2. Every sentence is cited with owner and last-reviewed date.
3. No personal diagnosis, dosing, legal or unpublished-data answers; we refer you to the 1922 hotline.
4. Epidemic status comes only from the human-published situation layer.
5. Superseded versions are excluded automatically.
6. Each answer has an audit ID; generative mode discloses provider (Anthropic) and model; errors can be reported.
7. Personal data in questions is masked before processing.

## Logs, pause and transparency
De-identified logs are kept for **12 months** and not used for training. OASIS can pause AI answers within **15 minutes**. A transparency report is published every quarter.

## Crawlers and external AI
You are welcome to cite disease pages, FAQs, news, clarifications, vaccine pages and open data under the Open Government Data License v1.0. Please include the source URL, owner and last-reviewed date, and do not cite superseded versions. See /llms.txt, page .md versions and the /v1/ API; respect robots.txt and keep to 2 requests per second.
