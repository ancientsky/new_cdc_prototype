Taiwan CDC carries out the functions of Taiwan's National Focal Point under the International Health Regulations (IHR 2005). The focal point is staffed 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, so that urgent public health events can be assessed, notified and followed up without delay.

What the focal point does

  • Event notification — assesses events that may constitute a public health emergency of international concern (using the IHR Annex 2 decision instrument) and communicates them to WHO through the IHR channel.
  • Verification — answers verification requests from WHO and IHR focal points of other countries and areas.
  • Information sharing — exchanges case and contact information for cross-border events (for example, travellers on the same flight or ship).
  • Points of entry — coordinates with airport and seaport quarantine stations on ill travellers and conveyances.

How to reach the focal point

Purpose Channel
Urgent event, any time (24/7) Taiwan CDC hotline 1922 (toll-free 0800-001922; from abroad +886-800-001922)
Official correspondence (office hours) Taiwan CDC switchboard +886-2-2395-9825, Division of International Cooperation
WHO and other IHR focal points Through the established IHR contact channel; the email address is shared with counterparts through official channels

Legal basis

  • International Health Regulations (2005)
  • Communicable Disease Control Act (Taiwan)

Contact channels on this page follow Taiwan CDC's published hotline and switchboard; no personal names are listed. Points of contact are reviewed every 12 months.
