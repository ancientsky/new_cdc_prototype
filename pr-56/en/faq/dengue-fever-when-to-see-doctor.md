# How soon should I see a doctor if I have a fever and might have dengue?

> 權責單位：急性傳染病組 · 最後審閱：2026-08-02 · 下次審閱：2027-02-02 · 授權：OGDL-1.0 · ID：faq.dengue-fever-when-to-see-doctor · 依據：disease.dengue

The incubation period is about 3 to 14 days. If you have a fever and have lived in or travelled to an area with dengue, see a doctor within 24 hours and tell them. The 1 to 2 days after the fever breaks carry the highest risk of warning signs; return immediately for persistent vomiting, abdominal pain, bleeding or lethargy.
