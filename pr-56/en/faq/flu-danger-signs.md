# What signs mean someone with influenza should see a doctor immediately?

> 權責單位：急性傳染病組 · 最後審閱：2026-09-20 · 下次審閱：2027-03-20 · 授權：OGDL-1.0 · ID：faq.flu-danger-signs · 依據：disease.influenza

Seek care immediately for these **danger signs**: rapid or difficult breathing, blue lips (cyanosis), bloody or thick sputum, chest pain, altered consciousness or low blood pressure.

In children also watch for high fever lasting more than 3 days, seizures, fast breathing, marked loss of activity or refusing to drink. These may signal severe complications of influenza.
