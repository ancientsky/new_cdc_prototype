Disease reporting

Time limits, process and system for reporting notifiable diseases. The table is generated from the disease master data and updates with it.

Professional version

Public: suspected cluster

If several people in the same place (school, facility, workplace, community) develop similar symptoms within a short time, call the hotline 1922

Reporting system (NIDRS)

Physicians and medical institutions report online through the Notifiable Infectious Disease Reporting System.

Open the reporting system ↗

External system requiring a health-professional account; this site does not handle reports.

Professional question

Answered in professional mode, citing legal provisions and effective dates.

Reporting process

  1. 1

    Step 1：Check the case definition

    Use the current case definition on the disease page or document library; a suspected case is enough.

  2. 2

    Step 2：Report in NIDRS

    Enter case details, symptoms and laboratory results.

  3. 3

    Step 3：Meet the time limit

    Report within the time limit given for the category and disease below.

  4. 4

    Step 4：Submission and follow-up

    Submit specimens as described under Laboratory testing and cooperate with epidemiological investigation.
