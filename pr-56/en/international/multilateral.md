# Multilateral cooperation: WHO, WHA, APEC and GHSA

> 權責單位：企劃組 · 最後審閱：2026-10-01 · 下次審閱：2027-10-01 · 授權：OGDL-1.0 · ID：page.international-multilateral

Taiwan CDC takes part in regional and global health cooperation wherever it can contribute expertise and receive timely information.

- **World Health Organization (WHO)** — technical exchanges with WHO experts and participation in WHO technical meetings when invited; Taiwan CDC follows WHO guidance and shares surveillance data relevant to global health security.
- **World Health Assembly (WHA) week** — Taiwan CDC experts hold technical exchanges with health officials and experts from partner countries during the WHA period in Geneva.
- **APEC Health Working Group** — joins work on emerging infectious diseases, preparedness and health security in the Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation forum.
- **Global Health Security Agenda (GHSA)** — aligns domestic capacity building with GHSA action packages such as surveillance, laboratory systems, workforce development and antimicrobial resistance.
- **Global outbreak alert and response** — follows global outbreak alerts and offers field epidemiology and laboratory expertise to partners when requested.
- **Global Cooperation and Training Framework (GCTF)** — co-organises international workshops on public health topics such as dengue, antimicrobial resistance and laboratory diagnosis.

> The list describes ongoing areas of cooperation; participation in specific meetings depends on each year's invitations and arrangements.
