# Global health security: AMR, vaccines and border quarantine

> 權責單位：企劃組 · 最後審閱：2026-10-01 · 下次審閱：2027-10-01 · 授權：OGDL-1.0 · ID：page.international-global-health-security

Global health security depends on what every partner can detect, prevent and respond to. Taiwan CDC contributes in three areas.

- **Antimicrobial resistance (AMR)** — One Health surveillance of resistant organisms in hospitals and the community, stewardship programs and expert exchanges; see the [AMR and One Health hub](/topics/amr-one-health/).
- **Vaccines** — vaccine-preventable disease surveillance, cooperation on vaccine supply during emergencies, and sharing of immunization program experience; see [vaccines](/vaccines/).
- **Border quarantine** — core capacities at designated airports and seaports under the IHR, cooperation on ill travellers and conveyances, and travel health notices for destinations; see [travel health notices](/travel/).

> These descriptions summarise ongoing work; detailed agreements are listed on the [bilateral cooperation page](/international/bilateral/).
