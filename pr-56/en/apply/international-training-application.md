# Apply for Taiwan CDC international training

> 權責單位：Division of Planning and Coordination · 最後審閱：2026-10-01 · 下次審閱：2027-10-01 · 授權：OGDL-1.0 · ID：service.international-training-application · 類別：other

Foreign health officials and professionals can apply online for Taiwan CDC international training (FETP exchange, workshops, laboratory, TB-DOTS and dengue control); applications are reviewed within 30 days.

Taiwan CDC welcomes health officials and professionals from partner countries to its international training programs: FETP exchanges, international workshops (including GCTF), laboratory training, TB control and DOTS, and dengue control.

Apply online with the application form and a nomination letter from your agency or the partner representative office. The Division of International Cooperation reviews each application with the hosting division and replies within 30 days.

- 誰可申請：Health officials of foreign governments、Public health and laboratory professionals nominated by their agency、Staff of partner institutions under a cooperation arrangement
- 處理天數：30 個工作天
- 費用：Training is free of charge; travel and accommodation arrangements depend on the program.
- 聯絡：Division of International Cooperation, Taiwan CDC — switchboard +886-2-2395-9825 (office hours); 1922 hotline (from abroad +886-800-001922)

## 申請步驟

1. **Choose a program**（Applicant）（0 日）：Check the program list and annual schedule on the International training page.
2. **Submit the application**（Applicant）（0 日）：Submit the online form with the nomination letter, CV and passport copy.
3. **Review**（Taiwan CDC）（21 日）：The Division of International Cooperation reviews the application with the hosting division.
4. **Notification and arrangements**（Taiwan CDC）（9 日）：Accepted applicants receive an invitation letter and arrangement details; others are informed of the result.

## 應備文件

- [ ] Nomination or endorsement letter from your agency or representative office

## 常見問題

### Is there a fee for the training?

Training is free of charge. Whether travel and accommodation are covered depends on the program and is stated in the invitation letter.

### Can individuals apply without a nomination?

Applications normally need a nomination or endorsement from the applicant’s agency or a partner representative office.
