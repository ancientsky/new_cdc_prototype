FalseSep 10, 2026Spread via：LINE、Facebook、YouTube

Claims that flu vaccine causes flu or kills older people are untrue

Claim: 打流感疫苗反而會得流感，長輩打完會死，不要讓爸媽去打

公費流感疫苗為不活化疫苗，不含活病毒，不會因接種而得到流感。接種後常見反應為注射部位疼痛、紅腫，少數人有輕微發燒或倦怠，通常 1–2 天內緩解。

長者感染流感後併發肺炎、住院與死亡的風險，遠高於接種疫苗的風險。我國設有預防接種受害救濟制度並持續監測疫苗安全，請放心接種。

Shareable official clarification

Taiwan CDC: the government-funded flu vaccine is inactivated and contains no live virus, so it cannot give you flu. Common reactions are a sore arm or mild fever lasting 1–2 days. Influenza is far more dangerous for older people than the vaccine. Get vaccinated from 1 October. Questions: call 1922.

Report to：1922 防疫專線、LINE 檢舉 · Responsible unit Public Relations Office · Last reviewed Sep 10, 2026

Partly trueMay 12, 2026Spread via：LINE、Facebook

Claim that all adults need another MMR dose is partly true

Claim: 日本麻疹大流行，所有大人出國前都一定要再補打一劑 MMR

日本確實自 2026 年 5 月 11 日起列為麻疹第一級旅遊疫情建議，但不是所有成人都需要補打。

依現行 MMR 預防接種建議（2025 年 4 月 16 日修訂），建議評估的是 1966 年（含）以後出生、不具麻疹免疫力、計畫前往流行地區的成人，請於出國前 2–4 週至旅遊醫學門診評估自費接種 1 劑。曾完成 2 劑、抗體陽性或曾確診者已具免疫力；1966 年以前出生者多數已因自然感染具免疫力。

Shareable official clarification

Taiwan CDC: not every adult needs another MMR dose. People born in 1966 or later without measles immunity who are travelling to affected areas such as Japan should see a travel clinic 2–4 weeks before departure. If you have had 2 doses or a positive antibody test, you do not need it.

Report to：1922 防疫專線 · Responsible unit Public Relations Office · Last reviewed May 14, 2026

Partly trueApr 16, 2026Spread via：LINE

Claim that only children get enterovirus is partly true

Claim: 腸病毒只有小孩會得，大人不會被傳染，所以大人不用洗手

腸病毒重症確實以 5 歲以下幼兒為主，但成人也會感染，多數症狀輕微甚至沒有症狀，卻可能把病毒帶回家傳給幼兒。

家有幼兒的大人回家後應先以肥皂洗手、更換衣物再抱小孩；照顧生病幼兒前後也要洗手。

Shareable official clarification

Taiwan CDC: severe enterovirus mostly affects children under 5, but adults can be infected too, often without symptoms, and bring the virus home. Adults should wash hands with soap and change clothes before holding young children.

Report to：1922 防疫專線 · Responsible unit Public Relations Office · Last reviewed Apr 22, 2026

OutdatedMar 20, 2026Spread via：LINE、Facebook

Claim that COVID-19 cases must isolate for 7 days with household members is outdated

Claim: 確診新冠要居家隔離 7 天，同住家人也要一起隔離，不能上班上課

居家隔離與同住家人居家檢疫是疫情期間的舊規定。自 2023 年 3 月 20 日起，新冠改列第四類法定傳染病「新冠併發重症」，輕症確診者不需隔離，同住家人也不需居家檢疫。

現行建議為「有症狀戴口罩、生病在家休息」：輕症者採自主健康管理，症狀緩解前避免探視長者或出入醫院、長照機構；高風險者確診後應儘早就醫評估抗病毒藥物。

Shareable official clarification

Taiwan CDC: mandatory 7-day isolation for cases and quarantine for household members ended on 20 March 2023. Mild cases no longer isolate; wear a mask if symptomatic, rest at home when ill and avoid visiting older people. High-risk people should see a doctor early.

Report to：1922 防疫專線 · Responsible unit Public Relations Office · Last reviewed Sep 5, 2026

FalseJan 15, 2026Spread via：LINE

No evidence that vinegar fumes or salt water kill influenza viruses

Claim: 在家燻白醋、每天喝溫鹽水就可以殺死流感病毒，不用打疫苗

燻白醋無法有效殺死空氣中的流感病毒，濃度過高反而可能刺激呼吸道，引發氣喘或咳嗽；喝鹽水也不能殺死呼吸道內的病毒，過量攝取鹽分對高血壓與腎臟病人有害。

預防流感最有效的方法是每年接種流感疫苗，並勤洗手、注意咳嗽禮節、保持室內通風。

Shareable official clarification

Taiwan CDC: vinegar fumes and salt water do not kill flu viruses, and vinegar fumes can irritate the airways. The best protection is an annual flu vaccine plus hand washing, masks when coughing and good ventilation. Questions: call 1922.

Report to：1922 防疫專線、LINE 檢舉 · Responsible unit Public Relations Office · Last reviewed Jul 1, 2026

FalseJul 15, 2025Spread via：LINE、Facebook

Claim that dengue spraying is deadly and can be refused is false

Claim: 登革熱噴的藥會毒死人，衛生所來噴藥可以拒絕開門

登革熱緊急防治使用的環境衛生用藥均經主管機關核准，依規定濃度稀釋，由專業人員施作。噴藥前會通知住戶收妥食物、餐具、寵物並暫時離開，依指示通風後即可返回。

依傳染病防治法，民眾應配合衛生單位的孳生源清除與防治措施。噴藥只能殺死成蚊，每週清除積水容器才是根本。

Shareable official clarification

Taiwan CDC: dengue control insecticides are approved, diluted to set standards and applied by professionals. You will be told to store food and leave briefly; return after ventilating. Cooperation is required by law, and removing standing water weekly matters most. Questions: call 1922.

Report to：1922 防疫專線、LINE 檢舉 · Responsible unit Public Relations Office · Last reviewed Jul 10, 2026

Claim that only people born after 1981 need MMR

Claim: MMR 只有 1981 年後出生的人要補打，1966 到 1980 年出生的人都不用

此說法依據的是 108 年 5 月版「國內現行 MMR 預防接種建議」，當時成人評估對象以 1981 年為分界。

疾管署已預告將於 2025 年 4 月 16 日修訂建議：屆時 1966 年（含）以後出生、不具麻疹免疫力的成人，計畫前往流行地區時也應評估接種。修訂公告後請以現行版建議為準。

Shareable official clarification

Taiwan CDC: "only people born after 1981 need MMR" reflects the old recommendation. From 16 April 2025, adults born in 1966 or later without immunity should also be assessed before travelling to affected areas. Follow the latest announcement.
