# Which adults should be assessed for MMR vaccination?

> 權責單位：急性傳染病組 · 最後審閱：2026-06-05 · 下次審閱：2026-12-05 · 授權：OGDL-1.0 · ID：faq.measles-mmr-adult-who · 依據：doc.mmr-recommendation

Under the recommendation revised on 16 April 2025, adults born in 1966 or later who lack measles immunity and plan to travel to areas with measles should visit a travel clinic 2 to 4 weeks before departure to be assessed for one self-paid MMR dose. Health workers and people in frequent contact with foreign nationals should also be assessed. The former 1981 cut-off no longer applies.

## 結構化欄位
- birthYearFrom: 1966
- assessWeeksBeforeTravel: 2–4
