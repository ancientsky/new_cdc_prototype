Health advice for travel to Monaco

歐洲 · Check the alert level, vaccines and care options before you go.

No targeted travel health notice Monaco · MC

Current level and notices

✓ No targeted travel health notice at present

Monaco has no targeted travel health notice at present. Usual precautions are enough.

  • Wash your hands often and take care with food and drinking water.
  • Avoid mosquito bites outdoors; do not touch or feed animals.
  • If you get a fever, rash or diarrhoea after returning, see a doctor and mention your travel history.

Also a global background reminder: COVID-19 with severe complications（Level 1）. Usual precautions are enough.

Your trip in three stages

  1. 1 Before you go

    • See a travel-medicine clinic 2–4 weeks before departure
    • No specific vaccine; make sure routine vaccines (such as MMR) are up to date.
    • International Certificate of Vaccination (yellow card)

  2. 2 While travelling

    • Wash your hands often and take care with food and drinking water.
    • Avoid mosquito bites outdoors; do not touch or feed animals.

  3. 3 After you return

    • If you develop a fever or rash within 21 days of returning, see a doctor promptly and tell them where you travelled and whom you met.
    • Call the 1922 hotline if you are unsure, and phone ahead before going to a clinic or hospital.
